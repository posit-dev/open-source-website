# Posit Open Source

> The home of open-source software from [Posit, PBC](https://posit.co) (formerly RStudio): Shiny, Quarto, the tidyverse, Positron, and hundreds of other open-source projects for data science, scientific research, and technical communication in R and Python. This site catalogs the software, the people who build it, and related blog posts, events, and learning resources.
