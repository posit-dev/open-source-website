We were proud to sponsor the Bioinformatics Community Meetup #5, hosted at Klaster Innowacji Społeczno-Gospodarczych Zabłocie 20.22!

A huge thank you to Bioinformatics Community PL for bringing together such an inspiring group of minds. Check out their event summary and photo highlights below:

## Bioinformatics Community PL Meet-up \#5 \- bringing together Poland's bioinformatics community

On 11 July 2026, more than 100 participants representing over 50 organizations from academia and industry gathered in Kraków for the fifth edition of the Bioinformatics Community PL Meet-up. The event provided a space for researchers, students, and industry professionals to exchange knowledge, discuss current challenges, and build new collaborations across the Polish bioinformatics ecosystem.

The scientific programme covered a broad range of topics, including computational approaches to targeted nanomedicine, viral genomics and the role of bioinformatics in virology, software architecture for clinical trial data, peptide function prediction using machine learning, research and education in bioinformatics, and strengthening collaboration between academia and the life sciences industry.

One of the highlights of the event was the expert panel discussion, "Reproducible Bioinformatics Research: From Academia to Industry," curated by PaperQC. The discussion brought together experts from academia and industry to share practical perspectives on reproducible research, software development, data management, and best practices that support reliable and transparent bioinformatics.

Beyond the talks, the meet-up offered extensive networking opportunities that encouraged new connections between researchers, students, and professionals from across the life sciences sector.

The organizers would like to thank all speakers, panelists, volunteers, partners, sponsors, and participants for helping create another successful edition of the Bioinformatics Community PL Meet-up. We are especially grateful to Posit PBC for supporting our mission of building an open, collaborative bioinformatics community in Poland.
