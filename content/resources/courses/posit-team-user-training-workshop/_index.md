<h2><span style="font-family: 'Open Sans', sans-serif;">Empower your data scientists with Posit Team User Training</span></h2>
<p><span style="font-family: 'Open Sans', sans-serif;">The Posit Team User Training workshop equips your data science team with the skills and knowledge to maximize their use of Posit Workbench, Posit Connect, and Posit Package Manager. Posit Team User Training teaches data science teams to integrate these tools seamlessly into their workflows, boosting productivity.</span></p>
<h3><span style="font-family: 'Open Sans', sans-serif;">Who is Posit User Training for?</span></h3>
<p><span style="font-family: 'Open Sans', sans-serif;">This workshop is perfect for data scientists, analysts, and other developers who are new to Posit Team or want to enhance their understanding of the platform. It is particularly suitable for individuals who use Posit&rsquo;s tools to create content using R and Python.</span></p>
<h3><span style="font-family: 'Open Sans', sans-serif;">What will be covered?</span></h3>
<p><span style="font-family: 'Open Sans', sans-serif;">During the workshop, learners will:</span></p>
<ul>
<li><span style="font-family: 'Open Sans', sans-serif;"><strong>Launch and manage coding sessions in Posit Workbench</strong> &mdash; practice running R and Python code interactively in Positron and automate longer jobs through Workbench Jobs for efficient, reliable execution.</span></li>
<li><span style="font-family: 'Open Sans', sans-serif;"><strong>Publish &amp; share work through Posit Connect</strong> &mdash; learn how to deploy reports and applications to your entire team securely and reproducibly.</span></li>
<li><span style="font-family: 'Open Sans', sans-serif;"><strong>Automate workflows with Connect</strong> &mdash; schedule recurring reports and analyses to save time and ensure stakeholders always have up-to-date results.</span></li>
</ul>
