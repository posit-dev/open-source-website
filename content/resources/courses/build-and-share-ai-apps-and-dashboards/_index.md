<p data-renderer-start-pos="918" data-local-id="abfc0217-7f16-43cd-b269-e6bf2c2e3afa">The Shiny and Querychat packages let R users build custom AI-based data science tools and then share them as interactive apps. This workshop will show R users how to build these apps and then share them on Posit Connect. Students will:</p>
<ul>
<li data-renderer-start-pos="918" data-local-id="abfc0217-7f16-43cd-b269-e6bf2c2e3afa">Use Positron Assistant to quickly build Quarto reports, dashboards, and Shiny apps</li>
<li data-renderer-start-pos="918" data-local-id="abfc0217-7f16-43cd-b269-e6bf2c2e3afa">Try out interactive Leaflet maps (interactive maps) and Datatables (interactive tables)</li>
<li data-renderer-start-pos="918" data-local-id="abfc0217-7f16-43cd-b269-e6bf2c2e3afa">Add a chatbot that analyzes and filters data to their app with Querychat</li>
<li data-renderer-start-pos="918" data-local-id="abfc0217-7f16-43cd-b269-e6bf2c2e3afa">Use Posit Publisher to publish their AI-Powered App to Posit Connect</li>
<li data-renderer-start-pos="918" data-local-id="abfc0217-7f16-43cd-b269-e6bf2c2e3afa">Control access to their app and securely store their AI credentials on Connect</li>
<li data-renderer-start-pos="918" data-local-id="abfc0217-7f16-43cd-b269-e6bf2c2e3afa">Launch the Usage Monitor App in the Connect Gallery to monitor their app</li>
<li data-renderer-start-pos="918" data-local-id="abfc0217-7f16-43cd-b269-e6bf2c2e3afa">See how Posit&rsquo;s thought leadership has provided a way to use AI that is safe, reliable, and reproducible.</li>
</ul>
<p data-renderer-start-pos="1776" data-local-id="e0c8a1da-a825-48b2-8ed5-de1e4ad4cb26">Participants will code in an environment provided by Posit that "just works" so students can experience the excitement of AI with no installation, setup, or security issues. We will provide a temporary AI API key for students to use on the day of the workshop.</p>
