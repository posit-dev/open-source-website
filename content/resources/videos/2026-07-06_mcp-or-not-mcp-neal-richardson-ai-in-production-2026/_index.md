description: "MCP, or not MCP? When the Model Context Protocol is the right tool for your LLM — when it isn't — and how to use it in a way that improves your security posture.\n\nPresented by Neal Richardson (Posit Software, PBC) at AI in Production 2026 — The Catalyst, Newcastle, 5 June 2026.\n\nABOUT THE TALK\nModel Context Protocol is a standard for defining tools that can be made available to your LLMs, whether in an IDE assistant, a command line utility like Claude Code, or other AI applications. There's a lot of noise out there about what you should use to get the best results from AI, and it can be hard to keep straight all of the new jargon that is thrown around. So in this talk, I will break it down and provide some guidance on when you should use MCP, and when you should probably reach for some other tool. I will provide some examples of cases where MCP is the right tool for the job, and when other things, like skills or other context files, are better. I will also devote attention to questions of security, as well as authentication, which are important for MCP. Some critics of MCP have argued that they are a security risk, and I'll discuss why that is.
