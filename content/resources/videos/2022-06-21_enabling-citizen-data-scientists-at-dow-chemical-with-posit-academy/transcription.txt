<h3>What makes a good cohort</h3>

<p><a data-t="1525">Okay, enough of the negatives.</a> <a data-t="1525">What makes a good cohort?</a> <a data-t="1525">I mentioned that a cohort</a> <a data-t="1534">needs to have somewhere between five and seven people in it.</a> <a data-t="1534">And Dow is a very large company.</a> <a data-t="1542">We have around 36,000 employees.</a> <a data-t="1542">I mentioned a few thousand within our R&D groups spread</a> <a data-t="1547">across the globe.</a> <a data-t="1547">This made picking our cohorts a bit of a challenge.</a> <a data-t="1547">What we've learned from this,</a> <a data-t="1554">we've had several now, is the components that make a good cohort are, number one,</a> <a data-t="1560">the people are from a similar work group.</a> <a data-t="1560">It doesn't have to literally be the same thing that</a> <a data-t="1566">people have the identical roles.</a> <a data-t="1566">In fact, we've found some good outcomes from having people at</a> <a data-t="1572">maybe some of our highest level of technical leadership roles and some people at the various,</a> <a data-t="1577">the earliest entrance into the company.</a> <a data-t="1577">Those seem to work okay together.</a></p>

<p><a data-t="1582">What is more important is that the types of problems that they're thinking about</a> <a data-t="1586">on a daily basis are similar enough that they can discuss with how they could apply the new</a> <a data-t="1590">skills that they're learning to their own work.</a> <a data-t="1590">Now, again, a nuts and bolts component here is</a> <a data-t="1598">you want to make sure that you have a similar time zone.</a> <a data-t="1598">You don't want people struggling to</a> <a data-t="1602">attend the group sessions.</a> <a data-t="1602">Those are critical to success, as we've heard from our participants.</a> <a data-t="1609">And then the third piece here, something that we sort of learned the hard way,</a> <a data-t="1613">of making sure that everybody within a given cohort is of a similar skill level.</a> <a data-t="1613">I had that</a> <a data-t="1619">long list of identifiers that might suggest that somebody is too skilled for Academy.</a> <a data-t="1625">You want to make sure that if you do have somebody with advanced skills participating, that</a> <a data-t="1630">they're well matched with people who are at least close to their skill level.</a> <a data-t="1630">You probably don't</a> <a data-t="1634">want a brand new beginner with somebody who's regularly deploying Shiny apps, for example.</a></p>
