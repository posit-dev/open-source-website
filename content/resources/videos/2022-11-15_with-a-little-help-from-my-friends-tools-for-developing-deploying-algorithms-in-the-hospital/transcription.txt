<h3>Authentication and scheduling with Posit Connect</h3>

<p><a data-t="1981">So, the first thing that we really need for deployment is authentication.</a> <a data-t="1985">So, all of our deployed applications run on </a><a href="/software/rstudio/">RStudio</a><a data-t="1985"> Connect, which is actually now Posit Connect.</a> <a data-t="1993">So, one of the nice things about Posit Connect is that it interacts with the hospital's active directory.</a> <a data-t="1999">So, what this means is that users can authenticate using their hospital username and password.</a> <a data-t="2004">So, for the developer, you know, we don't need to keep track of an extra server username and password.</a> <a data-t="2009">And then for the end users, in order to access applications, they could just log in with their hospital credentials.</a></p>

<p><a data-t="3187">The last thing I will say about monitoring is regarding clinical adherence.</a> <a data-t="3192">So how do we know that when a patient receives a chart watch alert, the clinician is actually doing something and they're not just ignoring that alert?</a> <a data-t="3201">So one of the things that we look at is the number of </a><a href="/software/vitals/">vitals</a><a data-t="3201"> that are measured after an alert is sent.</a> <a data-t="3207">So we do that because as part of the clinical workflow,</a> <a data-t="3211">one of the things that the clinicians have to do when they receive a chart watch alert is increase the number of vital measurements.</a> <a data-t="3218">This is something that we can easily measure just by looking at the electronic health care record,</a> <a data-t="3223">looking at the number of vitals that are sent after an alert has been sent.</a></p>
