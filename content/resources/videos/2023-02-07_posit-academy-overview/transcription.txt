<p><a data-t="608">And the way we fix this is by looking into why this happens.</a> <a data-t="612">So some of you may have seen this graph before.</a> <a data-t="615">It's called the forgetting curves or the ebbing house curves based on the gentleman who first</a> <a data-t="620">postulated it.</a> <a data-t="622">This summarizes a body of research that goes all the way back to the 1800s, has been verified</a> <a data-t="627">in many different fields.</a> <a data-t="629">It's just a fact of human life.</a></p>
