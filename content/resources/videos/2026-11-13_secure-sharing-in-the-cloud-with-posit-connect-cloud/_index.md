description: 'Sharing data science work means deciding who sees what: your team, your clients, or the public. Each needs different controls, and your security team will have questions about all three.


  In this session, Chris deMontmollin and Alex Chisholm from the Posit Connect Cloud team walk through the security work Posit shipped over the past year and why it mattered: SOC 2 Type I attestation, enterprise SSO, role-based access control, encrypted secrets, and custom domains. Then they go hands-on with a live demo of private, gated, and public sharing on Connect Cloud, and how to manage all of it as an organization.


  You’ll learn how to:


  Choose the right sharing mode for each audience and level of data sensitivity

  Share with clients and partners as external guests who only see what you choose

  Set up roles, permissions, and secrets so sharing scales with your team

  Answer the questions your security team will ask, including where to get the SOC 2 report
