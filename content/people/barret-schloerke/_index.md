`hello()`! Dr. Barret Schloerke is a Senior Software Engineer at Posit who has spent nearly two decades convincing computers to make data beautiful, interactive, and well tested — preferably all three at once. He builds and maintains the Shiny ecosystem for both R and Python. His newest project, [`shinyreact`](https://posit-dev.github.io/shinyreact/), pairs a Shiny server with a React front end so you get Shiny's reactivity with complete control over the UI.

He is an author of [Shiny for Python](https://shiny.posit.co/py/), where he built its testing infrastructure, bookmarking, and interactive data frames, along with [`shinyswatch`](https://posit-dev.github.io/py-shinyswatch/) themes. On the R side, he has helped shape [`{shiny}`](https://shiny.posit.co/r/), [`{shinytest2}`](https://rstudio.github.io/shinytest2/), [`{reactlog}`](https://rstudio.github.io/reactlog/), [`{shinylive}`](https://posit-dev.github.io/r-shinylive/), and [`{plumber}`](https://www.rplumber.io/), and the async and web server plumbing underneath them in [`{promises}`](https://rstudio.github.io/promises/) and [`{httpuv}`](https://rstudio.github.io/httpuv/). Most recently, he brought built-in [OpenTelemetry](https://opentelemetry.io/) support for production observability to Shiny on both platforms.

Beyond Shiny, his open-source portfolio spans AI tooling ([`querychat`](https://posit-dev.github.io/querychat/), [`chatlas`](https://posit-dev.github.io/chatlas/)), data visualization ([`{GGally}`](https://ggobi.github.io/ggally/), [`{trelliscopejs}`](https://hafen.github.io/trelliscopejs/)), and API tooling ([`{gqlr}`](https://schloerke.com/gqlr/)).
