`hello()`! Dr. Barret Schloerke is a Senior Software Engineer at Posit who has spent nearly two decades convincing computers to make data beautiful, interactive, and well tested — preferably all three at once. He builds and maintains the Shiny ecosystem for both R and Python. His newest project, [`shinyreact`]({{< ref "software/shinyreact" >}}), pairs a Shiny server with a React front end, combining Shiny's reactivity with full control over the UI.

He is an author of [Shiny for Python]({{< ref "software/shiny-python" >}}), where he built its testing infrastructure, bookmarking, and interactive data frames, along with [`shinyswatch`]({{< ref "software/py-shinyswatch" >}}) themes. On the R side, he has helped shape [`shiny`]({{< ref "software/shiny-r" >}}), [`shinytest2`]({{< ref "software/shinytest2" >}}), [`reactlog`]({{< ref "software/reactlog" >}}), [`shinylive`]({{< ref "software/r-shinylive" >}}), and [`plumber`]({{< ref "software/plumber" >}}). He also works on the async and web server layers underneath them, [`promises`]({{< ref "software/promises" >}}) and [`httpuv`]({{< ref "software/httpuv" >}}). On both platforms, he brought built-in [OpenTelemetry](https://opentelemetry.io/) support for production observability to Shiny.

Beyond Shiny, his open-source portfolio spans AI tooling ([`querychat`]({{< ref "software/querychat" >}}), [`chatlas`]({{< ref "software/chatlas" >}})), data visualization ([`GGally`](https://ggobi.github.io/ggally/), [`trelliscopejs`](https://hafen.github.io/trelliscopejs/)), and API tooling ([`gqlr`](https://schloerke.com/gqlr/)).
