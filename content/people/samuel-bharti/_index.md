Samuel Bharti is a computational biologist and a PhD candidate at the University of Alabama at Birmingham. He works on rare disease and cancer multi-omics, at present Neurofibromatosis Type 1 and the cancers it leads to, and on the software that helps researchers reach those results and explain them. He spent the summer of 2026 on the [Shiny]({{< ref "software/shiny-r" >}}) team, building 10 open-source applications and 5 packages for computational biology: [biobouncer](https://github.com/samuelbharti/biobouncer) for identifier validation, [biohttp](https://github.com/samuelbharti/biohttp) and [bioclients](https://github.com/samuelbharti/bioclients) for talking to biological databases, [plotomics](https://github.com/samuelbharti/plotomics) for visualization in R, Python and JavaScript, and [biocohort](https://github.com/samuelbharti/biocohort) for keeping a study organized. Most of them started as problems in his own research.
