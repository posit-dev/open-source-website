Samuel Bharti is a computational biologist and PhD candidate at the University of Alabama at Birmingham. His research focuses on rare disease and cancer multi-omics, with a current emphasis on Neurofibromatosis Type 1 and its associated cancers. He also builds open-source software to support biological research and make complex data easier to analyze, organize, and interpret.

He spent the summer of 2026 on the [Shiny]({{< ref "software/shiny-r" >}}) team, where he built 10 open-source applications and 5 computational biology packages. [biobouncer]({{< ref "software/biobouncer" >}}) validates biological identifiers, [biohttp]({{< ref "software/biohttp" >}}) and [bioclients]({{< ref "software/bioclients" >}}) connect to biological databases, [plotomics]({{< ref "software/plotomics" >}}) supports visualization across R, Python, and JavaScript, and [biocohort]({{< ref "software/biocohort" >}}) helps organize study data. Many of these tools grew out of problems he encountered in his own research.
